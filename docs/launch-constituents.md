## 2. Methodology

For each stock token, aggregated over every v4 and v3 pool that has it on one side:

```
V7, V30  USD volume over the 7- and 30-day windows, from Swap events, quote leg only
L        USD depth within +/-2% of price, from getSlot0/getLiquidity at head
P        Amplestocks' placement in that spoke                (--placement)
s        = P / (L + P)                                       capturable share
f        = 5 bp (SPOKE) or 10 bp (SPOKE_HIGH_VOL)            the fee we charge
R        = (V30 / 30) * s * f                                USD fee revenue per day
ROI      = R * 365 / P                                       annualised, on placed capital
```

`s` is proportional-share: a marginal dollar of in-range liquidity earns fees in proportion to its share of what is quotable at the touch. It ranks names correctly and over-states revenue — see §9.

**Volume** is the quote leg (USDG/USDC at $1.00, WETH at the WETH/stable pool price), so a swap is counted once. **Price** comes from `sqrtPriceX96` on the swap, not from the amounts, so fees and slippage do not bias it. **Depth** is the standard constant-liquidity band estimator: `amount1 = L*(sqrt(P_hi) - sqrt(P))`, `amount0 = L*(1/sqrt(P_lo) - 1/sqrt(P))`, both priced and summed.

**Filters and flags.**

- `high_volume` — top-quartile 30-day volume
- `thin_liquidity` — top-quartile turnover (V30/L)
- `dead` — no 7-day volume, or depth below the floor
- `no_feed` — no Standard Chainlink feed
- `multiplier` — uiMultiplier() != 1 (corporate action in effect)
- `insufficient_history` — too few daily prices for a volatility estimate
- `unpriced` — no USD price could be derived
- `api_delta` — chain volume disagrees with the aggregators by >40%

`dead` (V7 = 0 or L < $1,000) and `no_feed` are hard drops. Quartiles are measured over the live, feed-having population only.

This run's cut-offs: top-volume quartile at $46.50M of 30-day volume, top-turnover quartile at 2.48x/day, over a live population of 20.

**Weights.** `sqrt(V30 * L)`, clamped into the registry's own band for n = 12 (`cap_n` = 3,000 bp, `floor_n` = 416 bp) and normalised to exactly 10,000 bp. Rollout weights equal target weights. **Buy-fee bucket:** 10 bp when 30-day realised volatility exceeds 60%, else 5 bp. **Tick spacing:** the token's most common v4 spacing, else 60.

## 9. Caveats

1. **`R` is an upper bound, not a forecast.** The volume measured here is `<stock>/USDG` flow. An `AMPS/<stock>` spoke only sees a router when someone wants AMPS on the other side, or when an arbitrageur is closing a gap against NAV. Read `R` as *relative* attractiveness across names, and size the launch on the redemption floor, not on this number.
2. **Depth is a snapshot, volume is a window.** `L` is read at head; a book that emptied yesterday still shows yesterday's volume against today's thin depth and scores too well. The `thin_liquidity` flag is exactly where this bites hardest.
3. **The ±2% depth figure assumes constant liquidity across the band.** Where a tight range sits just outside the current tick, real depth is larger than reported; where the active range ends inside the band, it is smaller.
4. **`uiMultiplier() != 1` means a corporate action is in effect.** A split or spin-off changes what one token is a claim on. Names carrying the `multiplier` flag need the vault's corporate-action path exercised on 46630 before they are registered.
5. **SVR feeds are not Standard feeds.** Only non-SVR equity feeds count towards `no_feed`. If a ticker exists only as SVR, it is rejected here even though a feed address exists — `FeedRegistry.setStandardProxy` is the wrong call for it.
6. **Pools that appeared mid-window are under-counted.** A pool created on day 25 of a 30-day window contributes 5 days of volume against a 30-day denominator, so its `V30/30` understates its current run-rate. The per-pool `firstSwapTs` in `packages/quant/out/constituents.json` is how to spot them.
7. **Block timestamps inside the window are interpolated.** `eth_getLogs` returns no timestamps and a 30-day window is ~26 M blocks on a 100 ms chain; only the window boundaries are read exactly. This affects which UTC day a price sample lands in, and so the volatility and beta estimates, by at most the chain's block-time drift.
8. **Placement `P` is a parameter, and the repo now agrees with this run.** It ranks at $300, which is what plan revision 7's launch table produces: `S0` = 20,000 AMPS with a 45% POL tranche puts the per-spoke seed at **90 AMPS**, and at the auctions' $1.00 floor that is $90 of AMPS against a spoke ask ladder whose first cells sit at and above `P0` — the $300 default is the same 20,000/45% variant the brief specified and `packages/config/src/index.ts` (`launchParameters.supply.perSpokeSeedAmps`) now carries. The earlier disagreement was against the pre-revision-7 table (5,000 AMPS, 95% POL, 47.5 per spoke), and it is closed. Ranking was almost unaffected either way (`s` is monotone in `P`, and ordering only moves where two names' depths straddle `P`), but every ROI figure scales with the choice, so quote them at the `P` named in §1.
9. **This document is fixture output.** Nothing in §3-§8 is a market observation.
