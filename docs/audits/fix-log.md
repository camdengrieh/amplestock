## Revision 6 — the code paths earlier findings applied to, and what happened to them

Plan revision 6 (user directive, 2026-09-07) rewrote the fee model: `ampsFeeBps` on **both** directions of every
pool, the pass-through fee granted only to `AmpsRouter.rotate`, the creator paid in kind out of each currency at
`compound()`, the whole AMPS-side remainder burned, no staking. Several earlier findings and leads were about code
that revision 6 **deleted**. This section records which, so that a reader of the two audit reports can tell a fix
from a removal — and so that nobody re-fixes something that no longer exists.

### Removed, not fixed

| Earlier item | What happened |
|---|---|
| **The staking sandwich** (first-wave design note; doc invariant I36) — `AmpsStaking.totalAssets()` excluded `pendingRewards` so a same-block stake/unstake around `compound` could not capture a notified tranche, and I36 constrained the stream. | **The contract is gone.** There is no `AmpsStaking`, no xAMPS, no `notifyReward` and no reward stream, so there is no tranche to sandwich and nothing for a share price to accrue to. `AmpsVault` slot 6 and slot 2's `[16..47]` band are reserved holes where `staking` and `stakerBps`/`burnBps` lived. **I36 is deleted** rather than restated (`docs/phase3-state-model.md` §8.2); `docs/phase2-state-model.md` §1.3 records the removal in place of the old layout. `test/invariant/Phase2.invariant.t.sol` says so at the top of the file. |
| **`burnBps` as a governed share** (finding 6's neighbourhood; decision 6) — the burn was `(ampsFees − creatorCut − stakerCut) × burnBps / BPS`, bounded `≤ 2500`. | **There is no parameter left to bound.** The whole AMPS-side remainder is burned after the creator's slice, so the burn is a whole share rather than a fraction of one. `BURN_BPS_*` and `STAKER_BPS_*` are removed from `Constants`, from the launch parameter table, from the governance surface and from `docs/launch-runbook.md` §7's 48-hour list. |

## The fizz harness corrections

Campaign 6 over the merged revision-8 tree flagged two properties. Both are **harness statements**, not protocol
findings: the harness measured a quantity the contract does not divide by, and a supply fall the contract reports
in two places. A third, SP-10, is weakened from an equality to a lower bound by lead L-13(a) above.

| Property | Correction |
|---|---|
| **SP-14** `property_redemptionPaysAtMostProRata` | `redeemProRata` settles the inventory-burn stream **before** it prices anything, so the supply it divides by is `supplyBefore − drained`. Measured against the pre-settlement supply the pro-rata slice is too small by `drained / T` and the property fires on a redemption that did nothing wrong — the larger the pending stream, the louder. The basis is now `supplyBasis = o.supplyBefore − o.drained`, and the 25 bp of slack is unchanged, so what the property watches is still the *growth* of the reference-vs-pool valuation gap. **Deviation from the brief:** the brief writes the basis as `supplyBefore − (pendingBurnBefore + previewInventoryReleased − pendingBurnAfter)`; `o.drained` is that same drain taken from the **supply** (`(supplyBefore − supplyAfter) − shares`), which is arithmetically identical on the pre-L-13 tree and stays correct after it, because the queue also rises by fees a `view` cannot predict |
| **SP-24** `property_compoundBurnIsExact` | A `compound` settles the stream twice — once in `AmpsVault._beforePlacement` (finding 1) and once in the exit `_checkpoint`, which then finds nothing due — and that burn is `Burn(amount, "redeemInventory")`, not the `burned` figure `compound` returns. The bare equality is false for any `compound` run in the day after a redemption. The expected fall is `burned + settled`, with `settled` the fall in `pendingInventoryBurn()` across the call, captured in `ampsVault_compound`; a `compound` never queues, so the queue can only fall and the fall is exactly what the stream burned |
| **SP-10** `property_redemptionBurnIsExact` | Two changes. `drained` is derived from the supply rather than from the queue, because the queue *also* rises inside the same call; and the queue clause becomes `pendingInventoryBurn` rose by **at least** the preview's `inventoryReleased`, because lead L-13(a) queues the AMPS-side fees the removal realised and `previewRedeem` is a `view`. The ghost `queuedInventory` is measured as `pendingAfter + drained − pendingBefore` |

`contracts/PROPERTIES.md` and `contracts/fizz_data/property-plan.md` carry the restated wording, including every
sentence that said "the whole of `pendingInventoryBurn` is burned by `burnStreamStart() + D`" — which is now the
amount-weighted rule of finding 2 — and the pro-rata base, which is net of the standing queue since finding 4.
