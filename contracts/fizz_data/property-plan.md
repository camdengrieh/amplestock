## Merge and feasibility notes

**Non-mergeable properties, kept standalone as required:**
- **GL-25** carries `SPEC-05`, the `[MANDATORY]` V-06 first-depositor / donation-inflation property. It is
  *not* the same as GL-24 (zero-state safety): GL-24 asks whether the denominator and NAV stay well
  defined, GL-25 asks whether a donation into `A` can price an ordinary-sized bond out of existence.
  The template's literal "any deposit > 0 mints > 0" is false here by design (`ampsBonds_bond_dust`
  expects a 1-wei deposit to revert `ZeroAmount`), so it is stated at one whole collateral unit.
- **SP-46** is the C2 repeated-cycle dust-extraction property with its own dedicated round-trip handler.
  It is *not* GL-28 (a preview comparison) and *not* SP-45 (a single round trip); the wiring table
  carries `ampsVault_buyRedeemCycles` for it as required.

**Deliberate near-duplicates that were kept apart** (they assert different things, not the same thing twice):
GL-27 (a 1-wei redemption probe) vs SP-16 (a full-balance redemption never reverting);
GL-30 (the quoter's view of a same-pool round trip) vs SP-42 (the realised one);
GL-28 (split-redemption superadditivity on the preview) vs SP-46 (realised repeated cycles);
SP-01 (per-call mint attribution across every handler) vs SP-03 (where a bond's mint lands);
GL-40 (counter honesty) vs GL-41 (records vs the PoolManager's own books).

**Borderline feasibility — implemented, but marked MEDIUM or LOW and worth a getter check first:**
- `hook.rotationCredit(address)`, `hook.observationCoverage(pool)`, `hook.lastTruncatedTick(pool)`,
  `vault.assetsUsd18Of(address)`, `pot.budgetLeftRaw()` / `budgetLeftUsd18()` and `pot.usdScale()` are
  read by GL-37, GL-48, GL-47, GL-31/SP-20, SP-29 and GL-60. If any is not public, add a thin view or
  fall back to the ghost-only half of the property.
- **SP-28** needs `vm.load` on vault slot 15 plus a local mirror of `VaultRolloutLib._decayedMoved`;
  `load` is on the probed Medusa 1.5.1 list, but this is the only property that reaches into raw
  storage — hence LOW.
- **GL-19 and GL-41** walk 32 pools × 24 cells. If a fuzzer block will not fit them, give each a
  `uint256 seed` and check one pool / one asset per invocation: both identities are additive.
- **SP-16 / GL-27 / GL-61 / GL-63** must use the Medusa-safe shape (assert on the `catch` arm inside the
  handler); `vm.snapshotState` / `vm.revertToState` are **not** implemented in Medusa 1.5.1 and
  `Base.sol` records the probed cheatcode list.
- **SP-27, SP-59 and the ERC-20 half of GL-38 are SPECIFIC rather than GLOBAL on purpose**:
  `ampsVault_donateERC20` deliberately drops loose balances on the vault, the hook and the bond shell,
  and it is legitimate for one to sit there until the next entry point sweeps it.
- **SP-32 and SP-33** must be measured on *net* balances: `AmpsRouter._sweep` returns residual input to
  `msg.sender`, and the clamped handlers mint collateral with `fundActor` inside the same call — take
  the snapshot after the mint.
- **SP-11** must count ERC-6909 claims as well as ERC-20 balances: `redeemProRata`'s payout falls back
  to claims when an ERC-20 leg fails (fix-log wave-2 finding 1).
