The floor

Redemption pays assets, not a price.

Burn your AMPS and the vault sends you a pro-rata slice of every asset it holds — every stock token, every idle balance, every position — less a 1% fee. The code path reads no price feed, consults no gate, and cannot be paused by governance, the guardian or the timelock.

What it does not do is pay you a price. Their market value is whatever those assets are worth when you sell them, which may be less than the NAV shown when you redeemed.

The sell fee is charged on every AMPS-in swap in all 32 pools unless a rotation credit covers it.
